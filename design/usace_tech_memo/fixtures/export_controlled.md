# Purpose

This memorandum documents peak-flow frequency at the Cowlitz River near Castle Rock, Washington, for use in the Lower Columbia Stage Frequency Study.

# Summary

The recommended 1-percent AEP peak flow at Castle Rock is 226,000 cubic feet per second.

# Discussion

Bulletin 17C expected moments algorithm estimates are the adopted frequencies.

# Recommendation

The PDT should adopt the peak flows and stages in this memorandum.

# References

England, J. F., Jr., et al. 2019. *Guidelines for Determining Flood Flow Frequency—Bulletin 17C.* Techniques and Methods 4-B5. Reston, VA: U.S. Geological Survey.
