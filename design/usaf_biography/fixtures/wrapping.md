She leads the agency's collection, analysis, and dissemination of intelligence for air, space, and cyberspace operations, and she sets the standards the numbered air forces use when they train new crews.
  career: General Okonkwo entered the Air Force in May 1994 through the Air Force Academy. She has commanded at the squadron, group, and wing, served on the Joint Staff, and flown operational and training sorties from bases in the United States, Europe, and the Pacific. Prior to her current position, the general was the Director of Intelligence, Air Combat Command, Joint Base Langley-Eustis, Virginia.
  further: She is a command pilot with more than 2,900 hours in trainer, observation, and bomber aircraft. The general deployed as commander of the air component supporting operations at Joint Base Balad, Iraq.
