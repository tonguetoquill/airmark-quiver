# Project Authorization and History

## Project Authorization

The Flood Control Act of 1950 authorizes Lookout Point Dam as a unit of the Willamette Valley Project.

## Project History

Lookout Point Dam has regulated the Middle Fork Willamette River since 1953.

## Project Description

The spillway gates are the project's only means of passing the inflow design flood (IDF). Each gate is a hydraulic steel structure (HSS) on trunnion pins anchored into the piers.

## Service Disruption and Emergency Repair History

Gate 3 was out of service for 41 days in 2022 after a trunnion pin seized.

## Service Level Capability Change History

The gates' rated capacity has not changed since construction.

### Example Equation

The Westergaard equation gives the hydrodynamic pressure on the gates under the Operational Basis Earthquake (OBE).

$$ p = 0.875 W a_c \sqrt{H y} $$

**Where:**

- $p$ = hydrodynamic pressure
- $W$ = unit weight of water
- $a_c$ = the maximum base acceleration of the dam
- $H$ = the reservoir height
- $y$ = the depth below the pool surface

# Proposed Scope of Work

The work replaces the trunnion pins and bushings and recoats each gate.

## Gate Work

1. Install the bulkheads upstream of the gate.
2. Jack the gate and remove the trunnion pins.
3. Install the new pins and self-lubricating bushings.

| Gate | Pin condition | Coating condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Fair | Poor |
| 2 | Fair | Poor |
| 3 | Seized | Poor |
| 4 | Fair | Fair |
| 5 | Fair | Poor |

# Justification for Rehabilitation

A seized trunnion pin can keep a gate from opening during a flood.

## Reliability

The probability of unsatisfactory performance rises from 0.02 to 0.11 per year by 2040.

$$ P(t) = 1 - e^{-\lambda t} $$

# Engineering Considerations and Alternative Analysis

## General

The base condition is continued operation with emergency repairs.

## Alternatives Considered

The PDT compares pin replacement alone, pin replacement with recoating, and full gate replacement.

Pin replacement with recoating has the highest net benefits.
