This appendix documents the structural calculations for the regulating outlet gates at Cougar Dam.

# Design Criteria

The gates are checked for the usual, unusual, and extreme load cases in EM 1110-2-2105.

## Load cases

The usual case is the maximum regulated pool. The unusual case adds the Operational Base Earthquake.

### Pool elevations

The maximum regulated pool is elevation 1,699.0 feet NAVD88.

##### Record period.

Pool records start in 1964, the year the dam was closed.

###### Gauge datum.

The pool gauge reads NAVD88 since 2014.

# Hydrodynamic Loads

## Westergaard equation

Earthquake loads are based on an Operational Base Earthquake (OBE) with a 144-year mean recurrence interval. The Westergaard equation gives the pressure against the regulating outlet gates from earthquake forces.

$$ p = 0.875 \, W a_c \sqrt{H y} $$

**Where:**

- $p$ = hydrodynamic pressure
- $W$ = unit weight of water
- $a_c$ = the maximum base acceleration of the dam, as a fraction of gravitational acceleration
- $H$ = the reservoir height, to the bottom of the dam
- $y$ = the depth below the pool surface
