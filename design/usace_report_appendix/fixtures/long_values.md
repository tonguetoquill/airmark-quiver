# National Economic Development Benefits of Deepening the Channel From 43 Feet to 48 Feet Below Columbia River Datum

The benefit analysis follows ER 1105-2-100.

## Transportation cost savings for bulk grain, containers, and automobiles moving through the Portland, Vancouver, Longview, and Kalama terminals

Savings come from fuller loading of Panamax and post-Panamax vessels.

### Vessel operating costs for the post-Panamax bulk fleet calling at lower Columbia River terminals between 2030 and 2080

Operating costs are from the 2026 IWR vessel operating cost tables.

| Vessel class with a long descriptive name that wraps | Design draft (ft) | Hourly cost at sea ($) | Hourly cost in port ($) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Panamax bulk carrier, 65,000 to 80,000 deadweight tons | 44.0 | 1,850 | 1,120 |
| Post-Panamax bulk carrier, 80,000 to 120,000 deadweight tons | 48.5 | 2,410 | 1,460 |
