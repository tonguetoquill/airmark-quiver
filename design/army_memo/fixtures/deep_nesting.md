This memorandum sets the battalion standard for organizing written guidance. It applies to every staff section and company, and to every document a section publishes for others to follow, whatever its form or the office that signs it.

Staff sections organize their guidance by topic and by level of detail:

- Each section keeps a single index of its guidance, e.g., the standing operating procedures, the policy letters and the checklists it publishes, so anyone looking for a document finds it in one place without asking the section.
- Each document in the index follows the same outline:
  - The purpose and scope come first, then the responsibilities of each office the document names, and then the procedures themselves, in the order an action officer performs them during a normal duty day.
  - Procedures that branch are written as steps:
    - Each step starts with the action, i.e., the thing the action officer does, and ends with the result the next step depends on, so a reader new to the section can follow the procedure from start to finish.
    - Steps that repeat are written once and referred to by number.

Mr. Smith in the S-3 section reviews each index. Dr. Jones at the U.S. Army Records Management and Declassification Agency reviews the battalion index once a year.

Is the index current? Each section answers that question at its quarterly review and records the answer in its file plan.

Directive No. 5 governs the format of the policy letters themselves. Sections follow it as amended by para 2b(1). Then they submit each letter to the S-3 for review.

Sections keep drafts, working papers, notes, etc. The final version of each document is the only one filed.

Each section posts its index. (The S-6 grants access on request.) Sections update the portal within five working days of any change to a document in the index.

Companies follow the same standard for the guidance they publish, and the battalion S-3 reviews each company index at the quarterly training brief, together with the company training calendar and the company file plan.

The battalion S-3 publishes the first combined index by 30 November 2026.

Two exceptions apply:

- Classified guidance is indexed separately and kept in the battalion security container.
- Guidance from higher headquarters is indexed by its own number and is not renumbered.
