This memorandum requests space in the garrison records holding area for the inactive records of the 123d Signal Battalion. The battalion retires these records before the fiscal year closes, and the holding area is the only space on the installation that meets the storage standard for them.

Please confirm the space available by 13 November 2026. The battalion needs the answer by 13 November 2026 so it can schedule the move before the holiday block leave period begins.

The records in question fall into three groups:

- Unit files for the two companies that inactivated in fiscal year 2025, which hold their training, property and personnel actions.
- Signal operating instructions superseded during the last two fiscal years.
- Maintenance records for communications equipment that the battalion turned in during the last fiscal year.

The battalion packs each group in standard records boxes, labeled with the record number, the disposition and the date each box becomes eligible for destruction.

The battalion moves the boxes with its own vehicles and Soldiers, and asks for a two-hour window on a weekday morning for each of the three moves.

The holding area staff may inspect the boxes before they are accepted. Boxes that do not meet the packing standard return to the battalion for repacking.

After the transfer, the battalion keeps a copy of every signed transfer form in its own files and updates its file plan to show where each group of records is held.

The battalion appreciates the support of the garrison records team throughout this transfer and during the annual records review that precedes it. Questions about the transfer or the records involved may be directed to the battalion records manager, Mr. Robert L. Jones, at 915-555-0123 or robert.l.jones@example.com, who coordinates every move with the garrison staff.
