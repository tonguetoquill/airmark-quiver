This memorandum reports the Privacy Act records the battalion holds in its records holding area and the steps the battalion takes to protect them while they await retirement.

The holding area contains three groups of records that carry personally identifiable information: personnel actions for the two companies that inactivated in fiscal year 2025, the battalion family readiness group roster, and the medical readiness reports for the last two fiscal years.

Each group is stored in a locked cabinet inside the holding area. Only the battalion records custodian and the alternate hold keys to the cabinets, and the custodian logs every access in the holding area register.

The battalion retires the personnel actions to the Army Electronic Archive in the second quarter of fiscal year 2027. The family readiness group roster is destroyed when the next roster is approved, and the medical readiness reports are destroyed after two years.

Before any group leaves the holding area, the custodian checks each box against its box list and confirms that every box is sealed and labeled with its record number and disposition.

The battalion security manager inspects the holding area each quarter and reports the results to the battalion commander, together with any access the register shows outside duty hours.

The battalion trains every Soldier and Civilian who handles these records before they receive a key or an account, and repeats the training each year.

The battalion reviews the access list for the holding area each month and removes anyone who no longer needs access. The security manager keeps each month's list with the holding area register.

A custodian who finds a cabinet unlocked or a box unsealed reports it to the security manager the same duty day. The security manager reviews the register for the period and reports the finding to the battalion commander.

The battalion destroys records that reach the end of their retention period by shredding, under the supervision of the records custodian, and records each destruction in the holding area register.
