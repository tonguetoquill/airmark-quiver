The 123d Example Squadron moves unit physical training to three mornings a week on 2 November 2026. This paper gives the schedule, who it covers, and the decision in front of the commander.

PURPOSE

Give every Airman a standing time to train before the duty day, and keep the fitness-assessment pass rate at or above the wing standard of 90 percent.

SCHEDULE

- Monday, Wednesday, and Friday, 0615 to 0700, at the base fitness center
- Flights alternate the track and the weight room so neither is overcrowded
  - A Flight and B Flight take the track on Monday and Friday
  - C Flight and D Flight take the weight room those days, and the two pairs trade places on Wednesday
    - A profile that limits running stays with the weight room on every session
- Airmen on leave, quarters, or a profile train inside the limits of that profile

RECOMMENDATION

- Approve the schedule for 2 November 2026 through 31 March 2027
  - Director of Operations publishes the flight rotation by 24 October 2026
