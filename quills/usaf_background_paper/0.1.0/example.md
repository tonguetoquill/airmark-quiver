The 123d Example Squadron is deciding whether to move unit physical training to three mornings a week. This paper gives the background for that decision.

The squadron's fitness-assessment pass rate was 86 percent in September 2026, four points under the wing standard of 90 percent. The current session is at 1600, after the flying window, and flights miss it when the duty day runs long.

A session at 0615 gives every flight the same hour and keeps the fitness center from taking the whole unit at once.

- Monday, Wednesday, and Friday, 0615 to 0700, at the base fitness center
- A Flight and B Flight take the track on Monday and Friday, and C Flight and D Flight take the weight room
- The flights trade places on Wednesday

The Director of Operations can publish the rotation by 24 October 2026. The schedule would run from 2 November 2026 through 31 March 2027, and the commander is the approval.
