The 123d Example Squadron should move unit physical training to 0615 on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday. The commander should approve that schedule for 2 November 2026 through 31 March 2027.

The September fitness-assessment pass rate was 86 percent, under the wing standard of 90 percent. Training at 1600 falls after the flying window, and flights miss it when the duty day runs long. A morning hour is the same hour for every flight.

- The fitness center holds half the squadron when the flights split between the track and the weight room
- A profile that limits running stays with the weight room on every session

Approve the schedule. The Director of Operations publishes the flight rotation by 24 October 2026.
