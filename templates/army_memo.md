Write your paragraphs here. Each one is numbered for you.

- Start a line with a dash and a space for a subparagraph. It's lettered a.
  - Press Tab on the next one to set it under the one above, numbered (1).
    - Press Tab again for the deepest level, lettered (a).
- Press Shift+Tab to bring a subparagraph back up a level.

End with a paragraph naming the point of contact by grade and name, with their position, office address, phone and email.
