# Project Authorization and History

## Project Authorization

Provide pertinent information on the project authorization, including any modifications.

## Project History

Describe the project location and provide a vicinity map, plan and elevation of the structure as an enclosure. Provide a narrative description of the current operation and use of the project, the associated project benefits and the recipient of project benefits.

## Project Description

Describe the physical condition of the project and project features including an assessment of the engineering condition and reliability of each.

## Project Cost History

Provide a history of project cost. Describe and display on an annual basis the operation, maintenance, repair and rehabilitation cost history of the project.

## Service Disruption and Emergency Repair History

Describe and display instances of service disruption and emergency repairs. This should include all significant episodes of service disruption.

## Service Level Capability Change History

Describe and document quantitatively historical changes in the service level capable of being provided by the project. This information should be provided for at least the last 10 years.

# Proposed Scope of Work

Proposed Scope of Work (Including intent of repair as described in Major Rehabilitation and Major Maintenance of Inland and Coastal Navigation Structures White Paper)

# Justification for Maintenance

Describe the physical characteristics of all significant project features. Special attention should be given to features which have experienced unsatisfactory performance and which receive special emphasis in the report.

# Engineering Considerations and Alternative Analysis

## General

The base condition should describe an immediate or certain failure. Immediate and full scheduled maintenance should not be the only project alternative considered.

## Alternatives Considered

Discuss the alternatives considered. Present the results of the analysis for each alternative.
