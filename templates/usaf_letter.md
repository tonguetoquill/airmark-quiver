<!--------------
In the data above, edit:
  - Letterhead (your unit's full name below the first line)
  - From (your rank, name, duty title, and mailing address)
  - For (the recipient's rank, name, duty title, and mailing address)
  - Salutation (for example, "Dear Major Smith")
  - Signature block (your name in uppercase, grade, and service; no duty title)
---------------->

Open with the reason you are writing. A personal letter conveys official information in a personal manner, so write as you would speak to the reader.

Give the details in the paragraphs that follow. Keep each to one idea, and indent nothing yourself: the letter sets the first line of every paragraph in for you.

> Use a block quote for lines that should stay as written,\
> like a list of names or an address.

Close by telling the reader how to reach you, for example at DSN 555-0100 or first.last@us.af.mil.
