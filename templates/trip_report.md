# PURPOSE

State why you went. Name the trip in a sentence.

# TRAVELER(S)

Rank, first name or initial, and surname. Add the position title when the travelers come from different offices.

# ITINERARY

Where you went, the inclusive dates, and the people you met.

# DISCUSSION

The objective, what got in the way, what you found, and any commitment the unit made. Match the detail to the reader.

# CONCLUSIONS/RECOMMENDATIONS

The findings, and the action you recommend. A bullet under this paragraph becomes a lettered subparagraph.
